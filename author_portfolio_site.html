About the Author

Madu McJoe Ifeanyi is a horror fiction writer with a passion for crafting intense, suspenseful stories that linger in the reader’s mind. Known for chilling twists and psychological depth, he brings nightmares to life—one page at a time.

Books

Whispers in the Black Room
Five strangers awaken in a decaying mansion with no memory of how they arrived. A message in blood warns: "You cannot leave until the house is full." As they unravel its secrets, a ghost child stalks them... and one of them may not be who they seem.
